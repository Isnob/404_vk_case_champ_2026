TikTok Shop Referral Fees in 2024 by Category
Updated at:
10/08/2026
AI Summary

Effective October 31, 2024 (GMT-8), referral fees drop from 6% to 5% for five jewelry sub-categories: Diamond, Gold, Jade, Platinum/Carat Gold, and Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald. Most other listed categories remain at 6%. Pre-Owned categories stay at 5%, and Collectibles/Pre-Owned sales above $10K are charged 3% on the portion over that amount.

Category Referral Rates
Effective October 31, 2024 00:00:00(GMT-8), we have lowered the referral fee from 6% to 5% for the following jewelry sub-categories:

Jewelry Accessories & Derivatives+ Diamond
Jewelry Accessories & Derivatives+ Gold
Jewelry Accessories & Derivatives+ Jade
Jewelry Accessories & Derivatives+ Platinum, Carat Gold
Jewelry Accessories & Derivatives+ Ruby, Sapphire & Emerald
Referral Fee = Referral Fee Rate × (Customer Payment + Platform Discount − Tax)
The Referral Fee calculation base includes the shipping fee paid by the customer.
